About Swify

Less time cleaning. More time living.

Swify began with recent college graduates who knew how hard it is to keep up with a home while juggling work, school and everything in between. Today we connect Los Angeles households with the city’s best independent cleaning professionals.

Our mission

One less thing to think about.

Our mission is to make high-quality home care accessible, efficient and reliable — so you can focus on what matters most.

FAQs

Questions, answered.

Who will clean my home?

An independently owned, licensed and insured cleaning business from the Swify network, matched to your home and request.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

Life happens. You can reschedule or cancel up to 24 hours in advance at no charge — just let us know.

How long does a cleaning usually take?

It depends on the size and condition of your space, but most standard cleanings take between 1.5 and 3 hours.

What areas do you serve?

We currently serve the Los Angeles metropolitan area. Enter your zip code when booking to check availability.

Do professionals bring their own supplies?

Yes. Professionals arrive fully equipped. If you’d prefer they use your products, just let us know.

What if I’m not satisfied?

Your satisfaction is our priority. If something wasn’t right, contact us within 24 hours and we’ll make it right.
