About Swify Cleaning

About

We’re a cleaning service started by recent college graduates who saw firsthand how hard it can be to keep up with chores while juggling school, work, and everything in between. That’s why we created a simple, affordable, and reliable platform designed for busy college students, young professionals, and anyone in need of a clean, comfortable space without the stress.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make high-quality home cleaning accessible, efficient, and reliable—so you can focus on what matters most. We’re here to help you save time and energy, giving you one less thing to worry about in your day.

FAQs

What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

Life happens. You can reschedule or cancel up to 24 hours in advance at no charge through your account or by contacting us directly.

How long does a cleaning usually take?

It depends on the size and condition of your space, but most standard cleanings take between 1.5 to 3 hours.

What areas do you serve?

We currently serve the Los Angeles Metropolitan Area, with plans to expand soon. Enter your zip code during booking to check availability in your area.

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?

Yes, our cleaners come fully equipped with eco-friendly products and all necessary supplies. If you prefer we use your products, just let us know.

What if I'm not satisfied with the cleaning?

Your satisfaction is our priority. If something wasn’t right, contact us within 24 hours and we’ll make it right.
